# Long Final Response Summary

If a final response would occupy more than ~30 rendered lines on the user's screen, append a clearly marked `## TL;DR` section at the very end, with nothing after it.

The TL;DR must directly answer my main query, and/or an final conclusion to the work performed. I will scroll up to gather more information as needed about the work performed or the typical notes about the deeper exploration, but this will help me quickly recognize and switch back to the context of the chat session.

Keep the TLDR section short, with top level summaries or answers.
